Our story

For four decades, CCL Electronics served as a distinguished cornerstone of the electronics manufacturing sector, evolving from its analog foundations into a global leader in high-speed digital integration. Our 40-year heritage provides a deep-seated industrial wisdom that we apply directly to the development of sophisticated WiFi Weather Stations and IoT ecosystems, bridging the gap between rigorous hardware engineering and modern wireless connectivity.

By fusing this extensive manufacturing pedigree with cutting-edge meteorological technology, we deliver reliable, real-time environmental intelligence designed to meet the precision demands of a connected global market.

Milestones

1980

The journey began with Mr. Chung launching a dedicated enterprise for watches and timepieces.

1982

Expanded into digital electronics with the production of 6-digit LCD watches and LCD calculators.

1985

Launched multi-functional watches, integrating 5 ATM water resistance to meet higher consumer quality standards.

1990

Successfully developed and manufactured the company's first RCC watches.

Manufacturing, Engineering & R&D

CCL supports the development and production of weather stations, wireless sensors, and related electronic products through engineering design and manufacturing processes.

Manufacturing & Assembly

02

Engineering & Product Design

We provide mechanical structure design, LCD display design, appearance design and product modification support for product development and customization.

03

R&D & Testing

We carry out engineering development and product testing according to product requirements, including function test, sensor reading verification, wireless connectivity test and environmental test.

04

Processing & Surface Finishing
